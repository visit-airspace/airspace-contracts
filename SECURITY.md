# Security

Please report vulnerabilities in the AirSpace programs privately by email to hello@visitairspace.xyz. Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

Please include:

1. The program, `airspace-escrow` or `airspace-deposit`, and the instruction involved.
2. A description of the issue and its impact.
3. Steps or a test that reproduce it.

We will acknowledge your report, keep you updated while we work on a fix, and credit you when the fix is published unless you ask us not to.

The escrow program is a fork of the Solana Foundation escrow program. Issues in code that AirSpace did not change can also be reported upstream at https://github.com/solana-foundation/escrow.
